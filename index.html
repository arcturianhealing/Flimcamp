About Film Camp

Lights. Camera.
Creativity.

Film Camp gives kids and teens the opportunity to become real filmmakers in a fun and collaborative environment. Students work together to create original movies while learning storytelling, acting, directing, cinematography, editing, and production skills.

Every camper experiences the excitement of a real movie set while building creativity, confidence, teamwork, and communication skills.
